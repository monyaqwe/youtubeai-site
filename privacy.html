Privacy Policy

YoutubeAi — last updated November 3, 2026

YoutubeAi is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), YoutubeAi accesses the owner's YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. The access is used only to:

  • upload the owner's videos and set their metadata, thumbnails and channel banners;
  • read statistics of the owner's channels and videos (views, subscribers, audience retention).

YoutubeAi does not access any other Google data and does not collect data about viewers or any other people.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens and channel statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer only. They are not stored on any server operated by YoutubeAi.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the locally stored tokens stop working; they can also be deleted from the computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YoutubeAi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YoutubeAi uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
