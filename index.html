YoutubeAi

YoutubeAi is a private, personal tool used only by its owner. It prepares short videos and uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels, and reads basic statistics of those channels so the owner can see how the videos perform.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, no sign-ups, and nobody other than the owner can use it. The only Google account connected to it is the owner's.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads videos the owner has created to the owner's channels, and sets their title, description and thumbnail.
  • Updates the channel banner of the owner's channels when the owner asks.
  • Reads view counts and YouTube Analytics data of the owner's channels.

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
